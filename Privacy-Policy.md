# Privacy Policy — Package Tracker Bot

**Effective Date:** October 20, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PackageBot ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use its Discord commands and features.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue using the Bot.

## 2. Information We Collect

The Bot stores only the information needed to provide its package-tracking features.

### Information collected

- **Discord User ID:** Used to associate tracked packages and settings with your Discord account.
- **Tracking numbers:** Used to retrieve shipment information and monitor delivery progress.
- **Carrier information:** Used to identify the shipping carrier when available.
- **Package status:** Stored to detect changes and provide status notifications.
- **User preferences:** Stores whether your command responses are configured to be public or private.

The Bot does not intentionally collect your Discord password, payment information, or the contents of your private Discord messages.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected by the Bot is used to:

- Retrieve shipment information from Ship24.
- Save and display your tracked packages.
- Check for shipment status changes.
- Send package status notifications through Discord direct messages.
- Store your response visibility preferences.
- Maintain and troubleshoot the Bot.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The Bot relies on third-party services to operate:

- **Discord:** Processes slash commands and delivers responses and direct messages.
- **Ship24:** Receives tracking numbers submitted for shipment lookups and provides tracking information.
- **CogitHost:** Hosts the Bot and may process technical information through its infrastructure and logs.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Information submitted to these services may be subject to their respective practices.

The Bot's operator does not control the privacy practices of these third parties.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Tracked package information and user preferences are stored in a SQLite database used by the Bot. The Bot is hosted on CogitHost.

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect stored information and access to the Bot's credentials. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 6. Public and Private Responses

The Bot provides a setting that allows users to choose whether supported command responses are public or private.

Public responses may be visible to other members of the Discord server. Private responses are intended to be visible only to the requesting user through Discord's private interaction responses.

Automatic package notifications may be delivered through direct messages. Users should avoid sharing tracking numbers publicly if they do not want others to see them.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Tracked package records are retained in the Bot's database until they are removed using the Bot's features or deleted by the operator.

You can use the `/untrack` command to remove an individual package from your tracked packages.

Your saved response preferences may remain in the database after you remove a package. To request deletion of other information associated with your Discord account, contact the Bot's operator using the contact method provided by the operator.

Some information may remain in backups or third-party service records according to their respective retention practices.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not specifically directed at children under 13. Users must meet Discord's minimum age requirements and any higher minimum age required by applicable law in their jurisdiction.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot's functionality or data practices change. Updated versions will be published at the policy's designated location, with a revised effective date where appropriate.
